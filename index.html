About the Competition

Technology with purpose. Innovation with impact.

Empowering students to create meaningful change through innovation. The competition offers a platform to explore real-world challenges and present technology-driven ideas that can improve lives and strengthen communities.

01

What is IEEE SIGHT?

IEEE Special Interest Group on Humanitarian Technology (SIGHT) is a global network of IEEE volunteers dedicated to applying engineering knowledge and technological innovation to address real-world humanitarian and social challenges. SIGHT works closely with underserved communities, non-profit organizations, and local partners to co-create sustainable, ethical, and impactful solutions. The initiative focuses on improving quality of life by promoting community-driven development, empowering local stakeholders, and ensuring that technology is accessible, inclusive, and socially responsible. Through interdisciplinary collaboration, IEEE SIGHT encourages students and professionals to transform innovative ideas into practical solutions that create long-term positive change in society.

02

Why This Competition?

We believe in empowering students to become innovators and changemakers who can create meaningful and lasting impact in society. This competition is designed to provide a supportive platform where aspiring minds can explore, develop, and present technology-driven solutions to real-world social and humanitarian challenges. By encouraging creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving, the competition helps participants transform their ideas into actionable concepts. It also fosters collaboration, social responsibility, and innovation, inspiring students to use technology as a tool for positive change and sustainable development.

Where ideas can make a difference

Competition Focus Areas

Projects should address real community needs and align with one or more of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 7

Affordable and Clean Energy

Projects focused on renewable energy solutions such as solar, wind, or other clean energy technologies to improve energy access, especially in underserved or rural communities.

SDG 3

Health and Well-Being

Technology-driven solutions aimed at improving healthcare access, disease prevention, medical support systems, or overall community well-being.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Innovative educational technologies or platforms that enhance learning opportunities, digital literacy, or access to quality education for marginalized or remote communities.

SDG 13

Climate Action and Environment

Projects addressing climate change mitigation, environmental sustainability, disaster resilience, or ecological conservation using technological interventions.

SDG 6

Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WaSH)

Solutions focused on clean water access, sanitation systems, hygiene awareness, or water management technologies for community development.

Before you participate

Eligibility & Rules

Review the participation requirements and competition guidelines before registering your team or individual entry.

01

Who Can Participate?

  • Open to all DIU and all other university students (undergraduate and graduate)
  • Both individual participants and teams are welcome
  • Team size: Maximum 4 members per team
  • Each participant can register for only one team
  • All projects must be original and developed during the event
02

Important Rules

  • All ideas must focus on humanitarian impact
  • Plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification
  • Submission format: PDF presentation or PowerPoint (max 10 slides)
  • Ideas must align with at least one of the focus areas
  • Shortlisted teams will be required to pitch their ideas in person

Your path to submission

Submission Instructions

Follow these four steps to prepare and submit your innovative idea.

01

Complete Online Registration

Fill out the registration form with your team or individual details. You'll receive a confirmation email with further instructions and submission guidelines.

02

Prepare Your Submission

Create one of the following formats to present your idea:

Presentation Slides8-12 slides (PDF or PowerPoint)
OR
Infographic PosterSingle page visual presentation
03

Record Your Pitch Video

Create a 3-minute video presenting your slides or poster. No need for high production quality - a simple voice-over explaining your idea will work perfectly.

Keep it clear and concise Speak confidently about your solution Highlight the social impact Stay within 3 minutes
04

Upload & Submit

Upload both your slides or poster and the recorded video to a Google Drive folder. Share the folder link with us using the submission form (provided after registration).

Presentation slides or poster 3-minute pitch video Public sharing link enabled Submission form completed

Important Reminders

  • Ensure your Google Drive link has proper sharing permissions (anyone with the link can view).
  • File names should clearly identify your team and submission.
  • Submit before the deadline: January 31, 2026.
  • Keep a backup of all your files.
